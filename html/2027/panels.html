Round table

A moderated panel with industry and academic panellists, on a question that divides the field.

The ICAT round table puts industry and academic panellists on stage for a moderated, unscripted debate. Audience participation is encouraged.

Round table topic: "Human autonomy in the age of autonomous systems"

Day 3 · October 2027 16:00 – 17:30 Plenary hall
Moderator

Moderator TBA

Panelists
  • Jeffrey H. Johnson, The Open University UK
  • Anca Sailer, IBM TJ Watson USA
  • Paolo Faraboschi, HPE AI Labs USA
  • Jeff A. Riley, Monash University, Australia

Autonomous systems are moving out of controlled factory floors and into streets, homes, hospitals, and critical infrastructure. As these systems become more capable, the question of who, or what, remains in control becomes central.

This round table explores the shifting boundary between human autonomy and machine autonomy. Industry and academic voices debate whether the goal of autonomy is to replace human decision-making, or to augment it. The discussion touches on questions of accountability, transparency, safety certification, and the long-term social contract between engineers and the public.

The audience is invited to participate in the discussion.

Past round tables

  • ICAT 2025: Artificial Intelligence vs. Augmented Intelligence, featuring Jeffrey H. Johnson, Anca Sailer, Paolo Faraboschi, and Jeff A. Riley, moderated by Kemal A. Delić and Maja Vuković.
  • ICAT 2023: AI: Future Trends and Challenges, featuring David Abbink, Petar Popovski, Abdulhamit Subasi, and Migdat Hodžić, moderated by Senka Krivić.
