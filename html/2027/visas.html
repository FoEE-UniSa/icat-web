Entry requirements

Visa information

Most visitors to Bosnia and Herzegovina enter visa-free. Check the details below and, when in doubt, ask your embassy.

All participants should check the official website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina to see whether a visa is required.

As a candidate country for EU membership, BiH maintains a visa policy similar to the visa policy of the Schengen Area.

Visa-free entry

Bosnia and Herzegovina grants visa-free entry to all Schengen Annex II nationalities, and to nationals of several additional countries:

Azerbaijan
Bahrain
China
Kuwait
Qatar
Russia
Turkey

Passport or ID card

Nationals of the following states may enter BiH with a passport or a valid identity card:

  • Member States of the European Union
  • States signatory to the Schengen Agreement
  • Andorra, Montenegro, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, Serbia, the Holy See and Switzerland
