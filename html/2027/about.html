About the conference

A legacy of more than fifty years

ICAT is the longest-running scientific conference in Bosnia and Herzegovina and one of the oldest in South-East Europe in its field.

The Organizing and Program Committees of ICAT 2027 welcome professionals from academia, research and industry across Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa and Australia to the 31st International Conference on Information, Communication and Automation Technologies. While many conferences have moved online, ICAT stays physical: people present results, exchange ideas, and start collaborations that outlast the conference week.

A brief history

  • 1973: A symposium on informatics under the name Informatika Jahorina, held in the mountains above Sarajevo, with international interest from its very first edition.
  • 1999: Renamed International Symposium on Information and Communication Technologies (ICT), bringing telecommunications into scope.
  • 2005: Broadened further to include automatic control theory and practice. The conference becomes ICAT.
  • 2007: One of the first conferences in the region to hold an online session for remote participants.
  • 2009: Papers begin appearing in IEEE Xplore®, with technical sponsorship from IEEE Control Systems Society and ACM.
  • 2013: Re-established as a full-fledged conference, with a size and reach that match the growth of the field itself.

Five decades on, ICAT still holds to the same idea: a serious, welcoming meeting place for the information, communication and automation community.

Scope

Original, completed and unpublished work is invited across four tracks:

  • Computing: computational intelligence, machine learning, big data, cognitive computing, software engineering, human–machine interaction, distributed computing
  • Control & Automation: control engineering, robotics, industrial and automotive automation, hybrid systems, mechatronics, motion control, factory automation
  • Energy & Cyber-Physical Systems: power generation, smart grids, renewable energy, cyber-physical systems, MEMS and nanotechnologies, transportation
  • Networks & Communication: wireless communications, IoT, optical networking, sensor networks

Proceedings & sponsorship

Accepted papers are submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore®, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements. ICAT 2027 is technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Control Systems Society, IEEE Industrial Electronics Society, and IEEE Systems, Man and Cybernetics Society, and is hosted by the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, University of Sarajevo.
