Getting there

Travel to Sarajevo

Sarajevo is closer than you think, and the city rewards the detour.

By plane

Sarajevo International Airport (SJJ) is located just 12 km from the city centre and is connected to many European cities through hubs such as Istanbul, Vienna, Munich, Zagreb and Belgrade. Low-cost carriers also serve Tuzla and Mostar airports, both roughly a two-hour drive from Sarajevo.

  • Airport website: sarajevo-airport.ba
  • Flight search: google.com/flights, skyscanner.net, edreams.co.uk

By bus

Sarajevo is connected to many European cities by direct bus lines. Centrotrans Eurolines connects Sarajevo with Austria, Belgium, Montenegro, Denmark, France, the Netherlands, Croatia, Germany, Slovenia and Serbia. Companies such as Biss Tours and Globtour also offer direct lines from many European cities.

  • Bus station: autobusni-kolodvor.com

By train

Rail travel is a suitable and safe option for low-budget travellers. Sarajevo is directly connected to Zagreb, and from there to the rest of Europe. A one-way ticket Sarajevo–Zagreb costs around 60 KM (~€31), return ~95 KM.

By car

Bosnia and Herzegovina is steadily developing its highway network. Speed limits: 50–60 km/h within city limits, 80 km/h on open roads, 120 km/h on highways. Drive carefully on regional roads; they are beautiful and winding.
