Welcome to ICAT 2025

The 30th International Conference on Information, Communication and Automation Technologies (ICAT 2025) will be held in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, on September 02-04, 2025.

ICAT is the annual conference organized by the Faculty of Electrical Engineering Sarajevo (ETF Sarajevo) since the early 1970's, creating a forum for scientists and practicing engineers throughout the world to present the latest research findings and ideas in the areas of computer science, information technologies, control systems, communication technologies and power engineering.

The Conference is technically co-sponsored by IEEE Control Systems Society, IEEE Industrial Electronics Society, and IEEE Systems, Man and Cybernetics Society.

Attendance is open, and registration fees are required for paper presentation/publication.
