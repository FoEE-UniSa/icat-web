ICAT 2023 General Submission Call for Papers

We seek original, completed and unpublished work not currently under review by any other journal/magazine/conference. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Computational Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data, Cognitive Computing, Software Engineering, Human-Machine Interaction, Distributed Computing...
  • Control Engineering, Robotics, Industrial Automation, Automotive Control, Hybrid Systems, Mechatronics, Motion Control, Factory Automation...
  • Power Generation, Smart Grids, Renewable Energy Systems, Cyber-Physical Systems, MEMS and Nanotechnologies, Transportation Systems...
  • Networks and Communication Systems, Wireless Communications, Internet of Things, Optical Networking, Sensor Networks...

ICAT 2023 Call for Tutorials, Workshops & Special Sessions Submission Proposals

We seek to extend the ICAT experience by tutorials, workshops & special sessions on selected topics given by renowned scientists and practitioners in their fields.

We therefore solicit proposals for tutorials, workshops & special sessions on topics relevant to the ICAT community.

We also solicit proposals to organize hackathons & industrial sessions co-located with ICAT.

Submit proposal

Proposals for tutorials / workshops / special sessions can be made by sending a proposal to icat@etf.unsa.ba with subject "ICAT 2023 Proposal - Tutorial / Workshop / Special Session".

The proposal has (at least) to contain:

  1. Title of the tutorial/workshop/special session,
  2. Name of the organizer(s) with their affiliation,
  3. Tutorial / workshop / special session description (abstract),
  4. List of potential contributors,
  5. List of potential reviewers for special session,
  6. Target group for workshop and tutorial.
