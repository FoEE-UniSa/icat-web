<h1>Getting around the City</h1>
<p>If you decide to use public city transport, you will have a tram, trolleybus, bus and minibus network that will allow
    you to reach any significant location in Sarajevo.</p>
<p>The city transport ticket is mandatory and you can buy it on kiosks (1,60 KM) or by driver (1,80 KM). The card must
    be annulled after entering the vehicle.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.gras.ba/">www.gras.ba</a></p>

<h3>Bus</h3>
<p>There is also a large number of bus and minibus lines linking the city centre with sloping settlements and the
    suburbs of Sarajevo. The most frequent bus line is the one that drives from the Town Hall to the Dobrinja settlement
    (Line 31E).</p>

<h3>Taxi</h3>
<p>There are several taxi companies operating in Sarajevo that provide transportation services at very affordable prices
    compared to other European countries. The starting price for taxi vehicles in most cases is 1.5 KM, while
    each additional kilometer is charged at 1 KM.</p>

<h3>NEXT BIKE</h3>
<p>Sarajevo offers the possibility of cycling around the city. Most of the city has marked hiking trails.</p>
<p>Unleash a bike through the terminal, mobile app or smart card and enjoy driving!</p>
<p>Nextbike is available in the following locations: BBI, Alta Shopping Center, Importanne Center, location of the Zmaja
    Street from Bosna 47 (Hotel Bristol), location in Novi Grad Municipality, Sports and Recreation Center Safet
    Zajko, Hotel Radon Plaza and Dobrinja.</p>
<p>30 minutes free ride every day, and after 30 minutes free of charge, for each individual bicycle rental up to 60
    minutes, a fee of 1,50 KM / h.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.nextbike.ba/">www.nextbike.ba</a></p>

<h3>RENT A CAR</h3>
<p>To rent a car you need to have an international driving license. Most international rent-a-car agencies, as well as
    domestic operations at the International Airport Sarajevo. Most companies will provide you with the car's request
    at the address.In addition to the car, you can rent a van, minibus or bus in Sarajevo.The rental price of the
    vehicle ranges from 50 to 500 KM, depending on the class you are looking for.</p>
