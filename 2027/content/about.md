The [Organizing and Program Committees](committees.html) of ICAT 2027 welcome professionals from academia, research and industry across Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa and Australia to the 31st International Conference on Information, Communication and Automation Technologies. While many conferences have moved online, ICAT stays physical: people present results, exchange ideas, and start collaborations that outlast the conference week.

## A brief history

{{< history >}}

Five decades on, ICAT still holds to the same idea: a serious, welcoming meeting place for the information, communication and automation community.

## Scope

Original, completed and unpublished work is invited across four tracks:

- **Computing**: computational intelligence, machine learning, big data, cognitive computing, software engineering, human–machine interaction, distributed computing
- **Control & Automation**: control engineering, robotics, industrial and automotive automation, hybrid systems, mechatronics, motion control, factory automation
- **Energy & Cyber-Physical Systems**: power generation, smart grids, renewable energy, cyber-physical systems, MEMS and nanotechnologies, transportation
- **Networks & Communication**: wireless communications, IoT, optical networking, sensor networks

## Proceedings & sponsorship

Accepted papers are submitted for inclusion into **IEEE Xplore®**, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements. Technical co-sponsorship by the IEEE Control Systems Society, IEEE Industrial Electronics Society and IEEE Systems, Man and Cybernetics Society is **pending confirmation**. ICAT 2027 is organized by the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, University of Sarajevo. Companies interested in supporting the conference will find the packages on the [sponsorship page](sponsorship.html).
