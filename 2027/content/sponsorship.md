Sponsorship arrangements are classified into five categories. Each category includes different advertising options during the conference and across its promotional activities and communication channels.

## General sponsor

Flagship visibility: named on all materials, opening remarks, and the stage backdrop.

## Platinum & Golden

Prominent logo placement, booth space, and a slot in the social programme.

## Silver & Friend

Logo in materials and on the website, ideal for regional partners and startups.

For inquiries about sponsorship arrangements, contact us via email: [icat@etf.unsa.ba](mailto:icat@etf.unsa.ba?subject=ICAT%202027%20Sponsorship).
