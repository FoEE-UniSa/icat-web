All participants should check the official website of the <a href="http://www.mvp.gov.ba/konzularne_informacije/vize/default.aspx">Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina</a> to see whether a visa is required.

As a candidate country for EU membership, BiH maintains a visa policy similar to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_the_Schengen_Area">visa policy of the Schengen Area</a>.

## Visa-free entry

Bosnia and Herzegovina grants visa-free entry to all Schengen Annex II nationalities, and to nationals of several additional countries:

<div class="row g-2 mt-2">
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Azerbaijan</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Bahrain</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">China</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Kuwait</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Qatar</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Russia</span></div>
<div class="col-md-4"><span class="badge bg-light border text-dark">Turkey</span></div>
</div>

## Passport or ID card

Nationals of the following states may enter BiH with a passport or a valid identity card:

<ul class="mt-2">
<li>Member States of the European Union</li>
<li>States signatory to the Schengen Agreement</li>
<li>Andorra, Montenegro, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, Serbia, the Holy See and Switzerland</li>
</ul>
