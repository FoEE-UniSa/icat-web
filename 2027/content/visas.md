All participants should check the official website of the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina](http://www.mvp.gov.ba/konzularne_informacije/vize/default.aspx) to see whether a visa is required.

As a candidate country for EU membership, BiH maintains a visa policy similar to the [visa policy of the Schengen Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_the_Schengen_Area).

## Visa-free entry

Bosnia and Herzegovina grants visa-free entry to all Schengen Annex II nationalities, and to nationals of several additional countries: Azerbaijan, Bahrain, China, Kuwait, Qatar, Russia and Turkey.

## Passport or ID card

Nationals of the following states may enter BiH with a passport or a valid identity card:

- Member States of the European Union
- States signatory to the Schengen Agreement
- Andorra, Montenegro, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, Serbia, the Holy See and Switzerland
