ICAT 2027 tutorials are half-day, in-depth sessions designed to give attendees a working knowledge of a specific topic: a methodology, a tool, or a current problem in the field. Tutorials are held in parallel on the first day of the conference, before the main programme begins.

## Format

Each tutorial is **three hours**, divided into two parts with a short break. Presenters are encouraged to combine exposition with hands-on material (code notebooks, data sets, or exercises) that attendees can take away. Tutorial-only [registration](registration.html) is available at a reduced fee; combined tutorial + main conference registration is also offered.

## Proposed tutorial tracks

The following tutorial themes are being considered. Final topics will be selected based on proposals received and on the composition of the programme.

- **Federated and split learning at scale**: practical engineering of cross-silo and cross-device systems, privacy budgets, deployment with PySyft or Flower
- **TinyML on microcontrollers**: quantisation, deployment with TFLite Micro, latency and power trade-offs
- **Formal verification for ML systems**: VNN-COMP, α,β-CROWN, properties beyond robustness
- **Building a digital twin in ROS 2**: sim-to-real pipelines, sensors, actuation, fault injection
- **Time-sensitive networking on Linux**: TSN configuration, scheduling, and integration with 5G
- **Reproducible builds for research software**: Nix, containers, and provenance in scientific workflows
- **Privacy-preserving analytics with differential privacy**: composition theorems, Opacus, real-world deployment

## Call for tutorial proposals

Tutorial proposals should include:

- Title and a 200-word abstract
- Presenter(s) with affiliation and a short bio
- Intended audience and prerequisites
- A short outline of the material to be covered
- Any software, data, or hardware requirements

Send proposals to [icat@etf.unsa.ba](mailto:icat@etf.unsa.ba?subject=ICAT%202027%20Tutorial%20Proposal) with the subject *ICAT 2027 Tutorial Proposal*.
