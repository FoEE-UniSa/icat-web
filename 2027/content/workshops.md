Workshops for ICAT 2027 will be held on the afternoon of the first conference day and on the morning of the last day. Each workshop is a half-day, interactive session led by domain experts. The full workshop programme will be announced once the call for workshop proposals closes.

## Tentative workshop themes

The following themes are being considered. Additional workshop topics are welcome; please submit a proposal via the Call for Papers page.

- **AI in critical infrastructure**: failure prediction and resilience in power grids, water networks, and transport
- **Edge ML and TinyML**: running machine learning on constrained devices, deployment patterns, hardware/software co-design
- **Safety and verification of autonomous systems**: formal methods, simulation, regulatory aspects
- **Privacy-preserving machine learning**: federated learning, differential privacy, secure aggregation
- **Industrial 5G/6G and tactile networking**: ultra-reliable low-latency communications, time-sensitive networking
- **Sustainable computing**: energy-aware algorithms, green data centres, lifecycle assessment
- **Quantum–classical hybrid algorithms**: practical use cases for near-term quantum hardware

## Call for workshop proposals

Researchers and practitioners are invited to propose half-day workshops on topics aligned with the conference tracks. Proposals should include:

- Title and one-paragraph description
- Organiser(s) and their affiliation
- Target audience and assumed background
- List of potential contributors

Proposals can be sent to <a href="mailto:icat@etf.unsa.ba?subject=ICAT 2027 Workshop Proposal">icat@etf.unsa.ba</a>.
