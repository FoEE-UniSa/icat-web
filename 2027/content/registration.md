## If you have an accepted paper

- At least one author of each paper must register and settle any overlength charges. A paper without a registered author is not included in the proceedings.
- One registration covers up to two papers.
- A student registration counts as a regular registration only when the student is the first author.
- Register before the [camera-ready deadline](dates.html), August 2027.

Preparing the manuscript itself — templates, IEEE PDF eXpress, the copyright form — is covered by the [submission guidelines](submissions.html).
