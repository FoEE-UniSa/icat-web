## Practicalities

- All rooms are equipped with an **LCD projector and a PC**. Bring your presentation on a USB stick in **.ppt or .pdf**; PDF is preferred for compatibility.
- Arrive at your session room **at least 10 minutes before the session starts** to check your presentation and load it onto the provided PC.
- Each talk is allocated **20 minutes**: 15 minutes for the presentation and 3 minutes for discussion, with buffer for transition.

## Advice from the chairs

- The session chairs keep strict time. Rehearse your talk to fit comfortably in 15 minutes.
- Slides should be readable from the back of the room: large type, minimal text, one idea per slide.
- A volunteer is assigned to each room; call on them if you need anything.
