### When and where is the conference?

13–15 October 2027 in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. The venue and negotiated hotel rates are announced with the second call for papers. See the [venue page](venue.html).

### Is ICAT online or in person?

In person. Sessions, workshops and the round table all take place on site.

### What language are papers and talks in?

English.

### How long can a paper be?

Up to six proceedings pages in the IEEE double-column A4 format, figures included. Two additional pages can be purchased for an extra fee. The details are on the [call for papers](calls.html).

### Which template should I use?

The official IEEE conference templates. Converting to them late is where most formatting problems come from — see the [submission guidelines](submissions.html).

### Is the review anonymous?

Single-blind. Reviewers know who the authors are; authors do not know who the reviewers are.

### Will accepted papers appear in IEEE Xplore?

Accepted papers are submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements.

### Do I have to register to be published?

Yes. At least one author of each accepted paper must register before the camera-ready deadline. See [registration](registration.html).

### Do I need a visa?

Most visitors enter Bosnia and Herzegovina visa-free. Check the [visa page](visas.html); if you need an official invitation letter for the application, email icat@etf.unsa.ba after registering.

### How long is my talk?

20 minutes in total: 15 for the presentation, 3 for questions, the rest for the changeover. The [presentation guidelines](presentations.html) have the rest.
