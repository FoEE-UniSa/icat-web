## Submission guidelines

Papers must be written in English. Full papers are up to 6 proceedings pages, including figures, in the IEEE double-column format (A4). Two additional pages may be included for an extra fee.

Standard IEEE conference templates and detailed formatting guidelines are available on the <a href="https://www.ieee.org/conferences_events/conferences/publishing/templates.html">IEEE conference template page</a>.

Papers must be submitted through the online submission system, PDF only. All manuscripts are checked for originality against major publication databases.

<div class="alert alert-warning mt-4">
<strong>Single-blind review.</strong> Reviewers know the authors' identities; authors do not know the reviewers'. Each submission is reviewed by the Technical Programme Committee and additional reviewers.
</div>
