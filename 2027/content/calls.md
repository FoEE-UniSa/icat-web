## Topics of interest

Papers are welcome on any topic within the conference scope, including but not limited to:

### Computing & Intelligence

- Computational intelligence & machine learning
- Big data, cognitive computing, software engineering
- Human–machine interaction, distributed computing

### Control & Robotics

- Control engineering & industrial automation
- Robotics, mechatronics, motion control
- Automotive control, hybrid systems, factory automation

### Energy & Cyber-Physical

- Power generation, smart grids, renewables
- Cyber-physical systems, MEMS & nanotechnologies
- Transportation systems

### Networks & Communication

- Wireless communications & 5G/6G
- IoT, optical networking, sensor networks
- Networked control, edge intelligence

## Submission guidelines

Papers must be written in English. Full papers are up to 6 proceedings pages, including figures, in the IEEE double-column format (A4). Two additional pages may be included for an extra fee.

Standard IEEE conference templates and detailed formatting guidelines are available on the [IEEE conference template page](https://www.ieee.org/conferences_events/conferences/publishing/templates.html).

Papers must be submitted through the online submission system, PDF only. All manuscripts are checked for originality against major publication databases.

> **Single-blind review.** Reviewers know the authors' identities; authors do not know the reviewers'. Each submission is reviewed by the [Technical Programme Committee](tpc_members.html) and additional reviewers.
