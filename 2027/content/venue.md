## About Sarajevo

Sarajevo is the capital of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the country's administrative, economic, cultural, educational and sporting centre. The city is surrounded by the Olympic mountains (Bjelasnica, Igman, Jahorina and Trebevic) and sits at an average elevation of 500 m.

For centuries, the borders of two great empires met here, and the city carries both: Ottoman bazaar streets and Austro-Hungarian facades, mosques, Orthodox and Catholic churches, and a synagogue within a ten-minute walk of each other. Sarajevo has witnessed the Sarajevo Assassination, two world wars, the Winter Olympic Games, and the longest siege of a capital city in modern history. It has kept its hospitality through all of it.

Most of the city's attractions lie in and around the Old Town (Baščaršija), easily reachable on foot from the conference venues and well connected by public transport.

- **Area:** 141.5 km²
- **Altitude:** 500 m above sea level
- **Climate:** Mild continental; October is crisp and golden
- **Population:** ~438,000 (four city municipalities)
- **Time zone:** Central European Time (GMT+1)
- **Power:** 220 V, 50 Hz
- **Water:** tap water is safe to drink

## Currency

The currency is the Convertible Mark (BAM, locally KM). Notes come in denominations of 10, 20, 50, 100 and 200 KM. The exchange rate is fixed to the Euro: **1.95583 BAM = 1 EUR**. Exchange at banks, exchange offices, or post offices.
