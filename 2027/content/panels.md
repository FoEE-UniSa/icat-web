Autonomous systems are moving out of controlled factory floors and into streets, homes, hospitals, and critical infrastructure. As these systems become more capable, the question of who, or what, remains in control becomes central.

This round table explores the shifting boundary between human autonomy and machine autonomy. Industry and academic voices debate whether the goal of autonomy is to *replace* human decision-making, or to *augment* it. The discussion touches on questions of accountability, transparency, safety certification, and the long-term social contract between engineers and the public.

The audience is invited to participate in the discussion.
