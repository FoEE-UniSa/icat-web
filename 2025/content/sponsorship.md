        <h1>Conference Sponsorship</h1>
        <p>
            We offer different sponsorship arrangements to interested partners. Sponsorship arrangements are classified to the following categories:
                <ol>
                        <li>General sponsor</li>
                        <li>Platinum sponsor</li>
                        <li>Golden sponsor</li>
                        <li>Silver sponsor</li>
                        <li>Friend of the conference</li>
                </ol>
        </p>
        <p>
            Each of the categories includes different advertising options during the conference and in the conference promotional activities and communication channels.
        </p>
        <p>

        </p>
