<h2>ICAT 2025 Call for Tutorials, Workshops & Special Sessions Submission Proposals</h2>
<p>We seek to extend the ICAT experience by tutorials, workshops & special sessions on selected topics given by renowned scientists and practitioners in their fields.</p>
<p>We therefore solicit proposals for tutorials, workshops & special sessions on topics relevant to the ICAT community.</p>
<p>We also solicit proposals to organize hackathons & industrial sessions co-located with ICAT.</p>



<div class="card">
<h5 class="card-header">Submit proposal</h5>
<div class="card-body">
<p>Proposals for tutorials / workshops / special sessions can be made by sending a proposal to <a href="mailto:icat@etf.unsa.ba">icat@etf.unsa.ba</a> with subject "ICAT 2025 Proposal - Tutorial / Workshop / Special Session".</p>
<p>The proposal has (at least) to contain:
<ol>
<li>Title of the tutorial/workshop/special session,</li>
<li>Name of the organizer(s) with their affiliation,</li>
<li>Tutorial / workshop / special session description (abstract),</li>
<li>List of potential contributors,</li>
<li>List of potential reviewers for special session,</li>
<li>Target group for workshop and tutorial.</li>
</ol>
</p>
