<h1>Registration Fees</h1>


<p>
For accepted papers, at least one author MUST register and pay for the overlength charges (if any). Student registration is treated as a regular registration only if the student is listed as the first author. A registration is valid for up to two papers, and provides access to lunches, coffee breaks and social events.
