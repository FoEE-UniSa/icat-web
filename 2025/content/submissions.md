        <h1>Submission Guidelines</h1>

        <h3>Instructions for Authors</h3>
        <p>Papers must be written in English. Full paper length (up to 6 proceedings pages), including figures, are allowed for each paper. Papers should follow the IEEE double-column format (A4 size) for ICAT 2025. Additional two (2) pages can be included for an extra fee.</p>
        <p>Standard IEEE conference templates are found here:</p>
        <ul><li><a href="https://www.ieee.org/conferences_events/conferences/publishing/templates.html">IEEE conference Template</a></li></ul>
        <p>Detailed guidelines for creating manuscripts are found here:</p>
        <ul><li><a href="http://www.ieee-ies.org/conferences/paper-format-for-conferences">Creating Manuscripts for IEEE IES Conferences</a></li></ul>
        <p>Authors are expected to submit only their own original works that is currently not under review somewhere else. The conference will verify the originality of manuscripts by comparing them with millions of other articles in databases worldwide. Manuscripts that violate IEEE's publication principles may be rejected. If the violation is deemed severe, then disciplinary actions may also be taken by IEEE.</p>
        <ul>
            <li>
                <a href="https://www.ieee.org/publications_standards/publications/rights/plagiarism.html">IEEE Policy on Plagiarism</a>

<h3>Camera-Ready Manuscript Submission</h3>
    <p>Final versions of accepted papers must be uploaded to EDAS for inclusion in the conference proceedings and to be submitted to IEEE Xplore®. <strong>The deadline is August 10, 2025 (hard deadline).</strong></p>
    <p>IEEE maintains strict formatting standards for all manuscripts published in the IEEE Xplore® Digital Library.</p>
    <p><strong>Registration is required for all participants of ICAT 2025.</strong></p>
    <p>Below are the instructions to submit the camera-ready manuscript.</p>

    <ul>
        <li>Please read the reviews of your paper and follow the reviewers' recommendations for revising it and preparing the final version. When you are done with the revision, please print out your paper and carefully proofread it.</li>
        <li>Validate your manuscript using IEEE PDF eXpress. Any changes made to a PDF after IEEE PDF eXpress conversion or PDF Checking may invalidate Xplore compatibility. All PDFs to be included in IEEE Xplore® should be validated through PDF eXpress for Xplore compatibility.</li>
    </ul>

    <h4>Instructions for IEEE PDF eXpress</h4>
    <h5>First-time users:</h5>
    <p>Enter the following information:</p>
    <ol>
        <li><strong>Conference ID:</strong> 66432X</li>
        <li>Your email address</li>
        <li>A password</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Continue to enter information as prompted. An online confirmation will be displayed, and an email confirmation will be sent verifying your account setup.</p>

    <h5>Previous users:</h5>
    <p>You need to follow the above steps but should enter the same password that was used for previous conferences. Verify that your contact information is valid.</p>

    <hr>

    <h3>Final Steps</h3>
    <p>When you complete the online copyright transfer process and submit the form, you will receive an automated confirmation email letting you know that the copyright sign has been completed successfully.</p>
    <p>Submit your validated PDF to the EDAS platform.</p>

    <p style="background-color: #fff3cd; color: #664d03; border: 1px solid #ffecb5; padding: 1rem;">
        <strong>Important:</strong> If you fail to strictly follow all the above guidelines, IEEE may refuse to index your manuscript in the IEEE Xplore, even though that work may have already been accepted at the conference for oral presentation.
    </p>

    <p style="background-color: #fff3cd; color: #664d03; border: 1px solid #ffecb5; padding: 1rem;">
        Please note that the authors are required to electronically sign the copyright form in the online submission system after submitting final versions of their paper.
    </p>
