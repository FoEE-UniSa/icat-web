<h1>Visa information</h1>

<p>All participants are required to check the official website of the Ministry of Foreign;Affairs of Bosnia and
    Herzegovina to see whether they are required to obtain a visa when entering Bosnia and Herzegovina:</p>
<p><a href="http://www.mvp.gov.ba/konzularne_informacije/vize/default.aspx">http://www.mvp.gov.ba/konzularne_informacije/vize/default.aspx</a>
</p>
<p>As an applicant country for membership in the European Union, Bosnia and Herzegovina maintains visa policy similar to
    the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_the_Schengen_Area"
           title="Visa policy of the Schengen Area">visa policy of the Schengen Area</a>.
    Bosnia and Herzegovina grants <strong>visa-free entry</strong> to all Schengen Annex II nationalities and it also
    grants <strong>visa-free entry</strong> to several additional countries:
</p>
<ul>
    <li>Azerbaijan,</li>
    <li>Bahrain,</li>
    <li>China,</li>
    <li>Kuwait,</li>
    <li>Qatar,</li>
    <li>Russia,</li>
    <li>Turkey.</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p>Nationals of the following states may enter into Bosnia and Herzegovina with a <strong>passport</strong> or a
    <strong>valid identity card</strong> proving identity and citizenship of:
</p>
<ul>
    <li>Member State of the European Union</li>
    <li>State signatory to the Schengen Agreement</li>
    <li>Andorra, Montenegro, Liechtenstein, Monaco, San Marino, Serbia, the Holy See and Swiss Confederation</li>
</ul>
